## Story

As a **developer**, I want an in-house PSG implementation, so that PSG music channels and SFX are correct.

## Scope

- 3 tone + noise, Sega LFSR taps, attenuation table
- Writes from 68k and Z80
- Catch-up stepping

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Unit tests: tone period, LFSR sequence, volume table
- [ ] Sonic 1 ring SFX PSG component sounds right

## Test plan

- Unit tests

## Notes

- `audio::Psg`, in-house: 10-bit tone periods counted every 16 PSG clocks (MCLK/15), periods 0/1 hold the
  output high, noise rates /16 /32 /64 / tone 3, 16-bit LFSR with Sega's taps (bits 0 and 3), 2 dB steps.
- Unit tests: tone period (10 flips in 10 x 0x100 ticks), periodic noise period 16, white-noise period
  57,337, attenuation ratio 0.794 per step and 15 = silent.
- The mixer box-filters the PSG over the 4-5 ticks of each native sample.
- **Not verified:** "ring SFX PSG component sounds right" (listening).
